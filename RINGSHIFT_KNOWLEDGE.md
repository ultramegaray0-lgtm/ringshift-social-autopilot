\# RINGSHIFT — AI KNOWLEDGE



\## GAME



Name: Ringshift



Genre: Puzzle / Logic



Platform: PC initially



Ringshift is an indie puzzle game where the player manipulates beams, rings, locks and special mechanisms to solve increasingly difficult puzzles.



The core experience should feel:

\- Clever

\- Satisfying

\- Visual

\- Easy to understand when watching gameplay

\- Increasingly challenging

\- Atmospheric and stylish



\## CORE GAMEPLAY



The player manipulates rings and beams around a circular puzzle.



Important mechanics include:



\- Mirrors

\- Portals

\- Amplifiers

\- Blocks

\- Half-Blocks

\- Prisms

\- Echo

\- Locks

\- Source beams



The exact behavior of mechanics must never be invented by the AI.



If the AI does not know how something works, it must say that it does not know rather than making up a feature.



\## POWERS



Ringshift is replacing the old hint system with powers.



The player equips one power before entering a level.



Using a power disables the coin system for that attempt.



Known powers include:



1\. Make Locks Bigger

&#x20;  Makes rim locks occupy more rim pieces, making the puzzle appear easier.



2\. UFO

&#x20;  Summons a UFO for a limited time to interact with eligible gimmicks.



Other powers may be added later.



Do not invent additional powers unless they are explicitly added to this document.

\## SOCIAL MEDIA STYLE



Do NOT sound like a corporation.



Do NOT use generic marketing language.



Avoid phrases such as:



"Revolutionary gameplay!"

"An exciting new experience!"

"The future of gaming!"



Prefer natural, short, curious posts.



Good examples:



"Can you see the solution?"



"I thought this level was broken."



"This one took way longer than it should have."



"There is only one solution."



"Would you have seen this?"



The gameplay/video should usually do most of the explaining.
