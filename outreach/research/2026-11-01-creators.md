# Creator research · 2026-11-01

What was checked by hand for the creator outreach, and how sure each finding is. `sources.json` holds the
machine-readable records; this page is the readable version. Nobody was contacted.

## How the research was done, and its limits

* **Discovery:** web searches restricted to youtube.com for recent puzzle-game videos (2026 puzzle round-ups, logic
  puzzle reviews, Next Fest puzzle demos, browser puzzle games), two public creator lists (Feedspot "Puzzle" and
  "Indie Games" YouTubers), GameDiscoverCo's survey of indie-discovery creators and a browser-game blog list. No paid
  tools, no lead lists.
* **Verification:** for each channel one of its own video pages (or its About page) was read for the channel id,
  upload date, the games covered and any address the creator wrote in the description. Only addresses written out
  in full and labelled by the creator ("Business Inquiries:", "Business Email:", "Contact:", "Contacto:") were
  recorded. Nothing was guessed, decoded or taken from a third-party list. YouTube's hidden "View email address"
  button was not used.
* **Limits:** YouTube rate-limited page reads from the research session (roughly one page a minute, many refused),
  its RSS feeds are blocked by robots.txt, and the repository has no `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`. So subscriber counts, upload
  frequency and engagement were **not** measured here. The pipeline now measures them through the YouTube Data API
  on every run once the key is set; until then the records are marked "research record" and every draft built on
  one waits in NEEDS_REVIEW.

## A rule that affects more than one creator

Higher Plain Games will not cover games "built on or using generative AI code or assets" and its form requires a
declaration. Other curators have similar rules (Games Y'all was already turned down for the same reason). Whether
any part of Ringshift was made with AI assistance is not recorded in either repository, so only you can answer.
Until `RINGSHIFT_KNOWLEDGE.md` (or `SOURCES.md`) records the answer, sources with such a rule stay on hold.

## What makes the rest draftable

1. Add the free `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` secret. Each run then re-checks every channel above (exists, subscriber count,
   last 15 uploads, median views and comments, a business address labelled in the creator's own descriptions) and
   searches for new channels. Olexa and Jugando Indies become draftable as soon as a recent upload is confirmed.
2. For channels with no written address (Game Design Library, Veteran Game, Games Puff, Aliensrock, Tasselfoot,
   Icely Puzzles, Indie Game Clinic…), open the channel's About page yourself; if YouTube shows you a business
   address there, run mode `add-contact` with the address and the About page URL. That is the only legitimate way
   to use those addresses, and it stays your decision.
