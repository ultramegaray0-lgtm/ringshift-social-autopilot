How it plays

  • Each ring carries beam sources that fire straight through the centre.
  • Rotate the rings. A level is solved when every lock on the outer rim is lit at the same moment.
  • The twist: a ring's own pieces never affect its own beam, because they turn together. Every mirror, prism or portal on a ring only matters for the other rings' beams.

Mechanisms, introduced one at a time

  • Mirror bends a beam 90°.
  • Block stops a normal beam outright.
  • Half-block stops a beam going one way and lets it through going the other.
  • Amplifier makes a beam strong enough to punch through other rings' sources.
  • Portal moves a beam to its paired mouth, same direction.
  • Echo portal sends a copy through the paired mouth while the original keeps going, so one source can light two locks.
  • Prism splits a beam into two branches that can light different locks.

What's in the demo: 110 levels

  • Easy, Normal and Hard ladders, 25 levels each. Easy starts with a single mechanism; Hard 15 puts five on one board.
  • Challenge (5), Space Orchestra (10), Meteor Attack (10) and Rocket Attack (10).
  • Every Easy, Normal and Hard level is hand-made and checked against the game's own beam logic before it ships.

Controls

Phone: drag a ring or use the on-screen arrows. Desktop: click a ring's track to select it, then drag left/right or use the arrow keys.
